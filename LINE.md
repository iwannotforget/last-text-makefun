# The entry

> At 1%, dying as I lived: ignoring your software updates since 2022.

One dying phone's last text, 67 characters. `main.ts` writes exactly this
text to `/output/entry.txt` when the entry runs.

Why this line: the 1% anchor and the epitaph frame ("dying as I lived")
make it unmistakably a final text from the phone itself, and "since 2022"
is the specific detail that lands the joke — this phone spent four years
refusing software updates and is unrepentant about it on its deathbed, so
the voice is one stubborn personality from the first word to the last. It
was picked over 13 other candidates scored against the judge's published
criteria in order (full scoring in the workspace NOTES.md); the runner-up,
a charger/power-bank love-triangle confession, lost on freshness because
charger romance is the territory every other entrant tries first.
